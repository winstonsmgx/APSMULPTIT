## Reading list by course

Standard textbooks and official guidelines, with one or two per course marked as the priority read. The list was compiled from memory without a library check, so confirm the latest edition before citing.

| Course | Priority read | Further reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile Application Design | [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines); [Material Design 3](https://m3.material.io) | Don Norman, *The Design of Everyday Things* (rev. ed. 2013); Steve Krug, *Don't Make Me Think, Revisited* (2014); [Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/); [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) |
| Digital Publication Design 1 | Ellen Lupton, *Thinking with Type* | Timothy Samara, *Making and Breaking the Grid*; Josef Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems in Graphic Design*; Robert Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style* |
| Digital Publication Design 2 | [EPUB 3.3 specification (W3C)](https://www.w3.org/TR/epub-33/) | Adobe InDesign user guide: interactive documents and EPUB export |
| Game Design | Jesse Schell, *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses* | Hunicke, LeBlanc & Zubek, "MDA: A Formal Approach to Game Design and Game Research" (2004); Salen & Zimmerman, *Rules of Play*; Csikszentmihalyi, *Flow* |
| Storyboarding | Steven D. Katz, *Film Directing Shot by Shot* | Giuseppe Cristiano, *The Storyboard Design Course*; Mark Simon, *Storyboards: Motion in Art* |
| 3D Animation Design | Frank Thomas & Ollie Johnston, *The Illusion of Life* (12 principles) | Richard Williams, *The Animator's Survival Kit*; Isaac Kerlow, *The Art of 3D Computer Animation and Effects*; Blender or Maya documentation |
| History of Fine Arts and Design | Philip Meggs & Alston Purvis, *Meggs' History of Graphic Design* | E. H. Gombrich, *The Story of Art* |
| Presentation Skills | Garr Reynolds, *Presentation Zen* | Nancy Duarte, *slide:ology*; Chris Anderson, *TED Talks: The Official TED Guide to Public Speaking* |
| Digital Copyright | Vietnam Law on Intellectual Property 2005 (amended 2009, 2019, 2022) | [Creative Commons licences](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/); WIPO, *Understanding Copyright and Related Rights*; Berne Convention |
| Scientific Research Methodology | John W. Creswell & J. David Creswell, *Research Design* | Ranjit Kumar, *Research Methodology: A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners*; APA Publication Manual (7th ed.) |
| Multimedia Product Design Project | Tay Vaughan, *Multimedia: Making It Work* | Design Council, Double Diamond framework; IDEO, *The Field Guide to Human-Centered Design* |
| Internships, Seminar, Graduation | Your own report, slides and supervisor feedback | Your university course syllabi (đề cương học phần) for exact topics |
