## Mock interview: 20 minutes

Run this with a friend or with Claude acting as the examiner. Answer aloud, time yourself, and note weak answers.

| Time | Part | Questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 min | Introduction | Introduce yourself, your major and specialisation. Why do you want to study in Germany? |
| 2–8 min | Graduation Project | Pitch, then 3–4 follow-ups from the Graduation tab |
| 8–11 min | Internships | What did you do? What did you learn? How is it different from university? |
| 11–17 min | Courses (2–3 picked at random) | One definition question + one "explain" + one "give an example" per course |
| 17–20 min | Connections | Which course helped your project most? Which course was hardest and why? |

### Sample course rounds

- Mobile Application Design: "What is a user flow? Draw one for login." / "How does iOS navigation differ from Android?"
- Digital Publication Design 1: "Why use a grid?" / "Fixed vs reflowable?"
- Game Design: "Explain MDA with an example."
- Storyboarding: "What shot would you use to show emotion?"
- 3D Animation Design: "Walk me through the 3D pipeline."
- History of Fine Arts and Design: "How did Bauhaus influence modern design?"
- Digital Publication Design 2: "How do you design navigation for an interactive magazine?"
- Digital Copyright: "Can you use an image found on Google in a client project?"
- Research Methodology: "Quantitative vs qualitative, with an example from your project."
